## Power-BI_Project_Sales_Forcasting_Dashboard

# Discription
The objective can be broken into following details components:

1.Dashboard Creation: Identify the KPIs, design an intuitively appealing dashboard, and add interactive visualization and filtering to allow users to explore the data at various levels of granularity.

2.Data Analysis: Provide valuable insights to business entities about the effectiveness of their sales strategies through visualization and charts.

3.Sales Forecasting: Leverage historic data and apply time series analysis to generate sales forecasts for the next 15 days.

4.Actionable Insights and Recommendations: The end goal is to share valuable insights and actionable information that can drive strategic decision-making and support the supermarket's goal of growth, efficiency, and customer satisfaction.
   

## Learning:

Incorporated data analysis techniques, specializing in the time series analysis, to delvier valuable insights, accurate sales forecasting, and interactive dashboard creation, driving business success.
